{{- /* Reports whether a page uses the capped article reading column. A regular page is an article when mainSections is unset, matching the single template's historical default. With mainSections set, only pages in those sections are articles. Home, taxonomy and other node pages are wide. Called by: _default/single.html, data/content-image-sizes.html */ -}} {{- $isArticle := .IsPage -}} {{- if and $isArticle site.Params.mainSections -}} {{- $isArticle = in (apply site.Params.mainSections "lower" ".") (lower .Section) -}} {{- end -}} {{- return $isArticle -}}
