## Who we are

Fixture Co., a fictional publisher built for the Strata theme's test
fixture, operates this site and is its data controller: it decides what to
collect and why.

- Contact: the contact form at `/contact/`
- Data protection contact: Fixture Co.'s maintainer, same form

## What we collect

A static build stores nothing about a visitor. Only three things leave a
trace, and none of them come back to us as a usable record.

**Server logs.** The host records each request — IP address, browser,
requested page, time — for abuse handling. Retention follows the host's
policy, which is short and full of connections no reader could ever make.

**Contact form submissions.** If you send one, it goes to Fixture Co.'s
maintainer and is read once, for the reply.

**Advertising.** The fixture declares an AdSense publisher ID so the theme
can assert the ad pipeline mechanically. The request reaches only the ad
stack the fixture ships headers for; no remarketing list is built and no
profile follows you out of this site.

## Cookies and similar technologies

| Name | Purpose | Set by | Lifetime |
|---|---|---|---|
| Browser session | Keeps your colour-scheme choice between pages | This site | Until closed |
| Google advertising cookies | Frequency capping and fraud detection | Google, through the ad loader | Until you clear them |

## How we ask, and how you withdraw

The banner names what this site may store. Withdrawal is the same action in
the other direction; no answer lives in an account, only in your browser.

## How long we keep things

- Server logs: the host's own retention window
- Contact form submissions: deleted after the reply
- Advertising logs: governed by Google's reporting periods, published at
  Google's privacy page

## Your rights

- Ask what we hold about you, correct it, or have it deleted
- Withdraw consent as easily as you gave it

This is a demonstration policy for the fixture the theme builds with — the
real shape to adapt lives in the example site's templates.
