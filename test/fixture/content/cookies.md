## What this page is for

Some storage runs the site. Advertising needs your choice first. The long
answer — who receives the data and what your rights are — is the
[privacy policy](/privacy-policy/).

## Necessary

Set as soon as the page loads. Regulators treat strictly-necessary storage
as exempt from the consent question.

| Name | Purpose | Lifetime |
|---|---|---|
| Browser session | Keeps your colour-scheme choice between pages | Until closed |

## With your choice

The banner asks how the advertising category should behave.

| Name | Purpose | Set by | Lifetime |
|---|---|---|---|
| Google advertising cookies | Frequency capping and fraud detection | Google, through the ad loader | Until you clear them |

## How to withdraw

Withdrawal mirrors the question the banner asks; nothing is stored in an
account. What Google keeps from pages already served is governed by
Google's own settings.
